About Bluecrest Federal Support LLC

Service-Disabled Veteran–Owned Small Business

Bluecrest Federal Support LLC is a Service-Disabled Veteran–Owned Small Business headquartered in Linton, Indiana. We provide federal agencies and prime contractors with dependable administrative, facilities, grounds, janitorial, logistics, and general operational support services.

Driven by veteran leadership and a commitment to excellence, Bluecrest offers broad multi-service capabilities designed to help agencies operate more efficiently, respond quickly, and meet mission objectives. Our team brings the discipline, communication standards, and reliability you expect from a veteran-led organization.

We support clients across Indiana and Nationwide, with the ability to respond to purchase-card micro-purchases, simplified acquisitions, and subcontracting requirements. Whether the need is short-notice staffing, facility upkeep, delivery support, or administrative assistance, Bluecrest delivers clear communication, consistent performance, and results that agencies can depend on.
