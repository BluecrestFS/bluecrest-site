About Bluecrest Federal Support LLC

Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business  ·  Linton, Indiana

Bluecrest Federal Support LLC is a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business providing grounds maintenance, janitorial, and facility support services for federal agencies and prime contractors across Indiana and the Midwest.

Founded by a medically retired U.S. Army veteran, Bluecrest was built around a straightforward premise: federal agencies deserve contractors who show up, communicate clearly, and finish what they start. Not because it's a selling point — because it's the standard.

We support federal agencies and prime contractors across the region with the ability to respond to simplified acquisitions, micro-purchases, and subcontracting requirements. Every engagement — regardless of size — gets the same level of attention, the same quality controls, and the same direct line to the person accountable for the work.

Mission

To deliver dependable, mission-aligned grounds maintenance, janitorial, and facility support services to federal agencies — with the discipline, communication, and accountability that the work demands.

Vision

To be the contractor federal agencies and prime contractors in the Midwest trust to show up, perform, and never make them wonder where things stand — building that reputation one contract at a time.

What Makes Bluecrest Different

These aren't talking points. They're the specific things we do differently — and why they matter on a federal contract.

💬

You Always Know Where Things Stand

Proactive communication isn't something we do when things go wrong — it's how we operate by default. COs and CORs get updates before they have to ask. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

🛡️

Military Standards Applied to Civilian Contracts

A decade of Army service doesn't just build résumé lines — it builds habits. Precision, accountability, following through when it's inconvenient, and treating every requirement as mission-critical regardless of dollar value. That background shapes how every Bluecrest contract gets executed.

📍

Local Presence That Actually Means Something

Bluecrest is based in Linton, Indiana — not a regional office, not a shell entity registered in another state. When a site needs attention, we're not coordinating from across the country. We're here, and our vendor network is built around that proximity.

👥

A Subcontractor Network Built on Standards

Every vendor Bluecrest works with operates under clear written agreements, defined quality expectations, and documented performance requirements. The government doesn't get a handshake network — it gets a managed one.

✅

Compliance Ready Before Day One

SAM registered, SDVOSB certified, insurance documented, internal SOPs in place — Bluecrest is structured to operate within federal requirements from contract award without the ramp-up period that slows other contractors down.

🎯

Direct Access to the Person Accountable

When you work with Bluecrest, your point of contact is the founder — not a project coordinator, not a customer service rep. The person who signed the contract is the person who answers when something needs attention.
