The Work

  • Clean and restock restrooms and showers
  • Empty trash and recycling
  • Vacuum carpet; sweep and mop hard floors
  • Dusting, interior glass, and drinking fountains
  • Report building issues to your supervisor

Supplies and equipment are provided.

Requirements

  • Able to pass an Army background check for facility access
  • Eligible to work in the U.S. (we use E-Verify)
  • Reliable transportation to the site
  • Dependable and detail-oriented; experience is a plus, not required

Veterans and military spouses are strongly encouraged to apply.

How to Apply

Email jobs@bluecrestfederalsupport.com with the location in the subject line (Terre Haute or Scottsburg) and include:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The town you live in
  • Which weekdays you are available
  • Any cleaning or custodial experience (a resume is welcome but not required)

Bluecrest will never ask for payment, bank details, or your Social Security number before a formal written job offer. If someone claiming to be us does, it is not us.
