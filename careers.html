Custodian – Terre Haute

Army Reserve Center · Terre Haute, IN

  • $21.79/hr
  • About 12–14 hrs/week · Mondays required
  • Begins upon contract award
View detailsHide details
$21.79/hr$16.37 base wage + $5.42/hr health & welfare, paid in cash on every check. Paid holidays, paid vacation, and paid sick leave.

Schedule

  • Every Monday is required. Restrooms, trash, and carpeted offices must be done on Mondays. If a Monday is a federal holiday, that work moves to Tuesday.
  • One more weekday (often a half day) for hard floors and monthly tasks.
  • Daytime hours only, 7:30 AM – 4:00 PM. About 12–14 hours a week total.

Regular duties (every week)

  • Clean, disinfect, and restock restrooms and showers
  • Empty trash and recycling throughout the building
  • Vacuum carpeted offices and classrooms
  • Sweep and damp-mop vinyl, tile, and concrete floors
  • Clean drinking fountains and utility sinks
  • Clean entrance mats and pick up litter within 25 feet of entrances

Monthly

  • Dust furniture, ledges, and fixtures (low dusting)
  • Clean interior glass and door windows
  • Sweep and mop storage and lower-traffic rooms

Twice a year (spring and fall)

  • Spray buff hard floors
  • High dusting (vents, lights, high ledges)

Once a year

  • Strip and re-wax hard floors
  • Shampoo / extract carpets
  • Deep clean restroom tile and grout
  • Wash interior and exterior windows

Deep-cleaning jobs are scheduled ahead of time with the facility and may occasionally run early, late, or on a Saturday. Floor machines, supplies, and training are provided.

Requirements

  • Available every Monday (see Schedule)
  • Pass an Army background check for access to the Reserve Center
  • Eligible to work in the U.S. (we use E-Verify)
  • Complete required Army security awareness training (provided by Bluecrest)
  • Reliable transportation to the site
  • Able to stand, walk, bend, and push a vacuum or mop for several hours
  • Dependable and detail-oriented; cleaning experience is a plus, not required

How to apply

Email jobs@bluecrestfederalsupport.com with “Custodian – Terre Haute” in the subject line and include:

  • Your name and phone number
  • The town you live in
  • Which weekdays you are available
  • Any cleaning or custodial experience (a resume is welcome but not required)
