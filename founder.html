About the Founder

Austin McQuistion

Founder & CEO, Bluecrest Federal Support LLC

🛡️ Service-Disabled Veteran, U.S. Army – Fire Support Specialist

Austin founded Bluecrest Federal Support LLC with one mission in mind: to bring clear communication, transparency, and accountability back into federal contracting.

After hearing repeatedly from government personnel about inconsistent service and poor contractor communication, he saw an opportunity to build something better — a company grounded in respect, honesty, integrity, and transparency.

As a former Fire Support Specialist in the U.S. Army, Austin understands the importance of accuracy, coordination, and mission-focused execution. Those principles guide how Bluecrest operates today. When agencies work with Bluecrest, they receive direct communication, reliable support, and a partner who treats their mission as his own.
