Austin McQuistion

Founder & CEO, Bluecrest Federal Support LLC

U.S. Army Veteran  |  Fire Support Specialist  |  Linton, Indiana

Austin McQuistion didn't set out to start a federal contracting company. He set out to serve — and did, for a decade, as a U.S. Army Fire Support Specialist. When a medical retirement ended that career ahead of schedule, Austin took the standards, the discipline, and the mission-first mindset he'd built in uniform and found a new way to put them to work.

Bluecrest Federal Support LLC is the result.

Why He Built It

Federal contracting has a reputation problem. Contractors who win work and disappear. Communication that stops the moment the contract is signed. Performance that looks acceptable on paper and falls apart in the field.

Austin built Bluecrest around a different standard — one he learned in an environment where falling short wasn't an option. Clear communication. Consistent follow-through. Accountability that doesn't require chasing anyone down. Those aren't aspirations at Bluecrest. They're the baseline.

What He Brings to Every Contract

A decade of military service teaches things that can't be learned in a classroom — how to coordinate moving parts under pressure, how to manage people and assets toward a defined outcome, and how to operate within strict requirements without losing sight of the actual mission. That background shapes how Austin runs Bluecrest and how Bluecrest approaches every contract — with the same attention to detail, the same intolerance for excuses, and the same expectation that the job gets done right the first time.

How Bluecrest Operates

Austin oversees every client relationship directly. When you work with Bluecrest, you have a point of contact who knows your contract, knows your site, and is reachable when it matters. Not a call center. Not a rotating account manager. The founder.

As Bluecrest grows, it will grow the right way — with people who hold the same standard, not just people who fill a seat. That's not a limitation. That's the point.

What Drives Every Decision

🎯

Accountability

💬

Communication

Proactive, direct, and consistent. You'll never have to wonder where things stand or chase down a status update.

Federal work has no margin for "close enough." Every requirement gets the same attention to detail — regardless of contract size.
