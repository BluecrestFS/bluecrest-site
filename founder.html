About the Founder

Austin McQuistion

Founder, Bluecrest Federal Support LLC

U.S. Army Veteran | Fire Support Specialist

Austin founded Bluecrest Federal Support LLC to build a company centered on clear communication, dependable execution, and accountability from start to finish.

After hearing repeated concerns about inconsistent contractor communication and unreliable service delivery, Austin saw an opportunity to build a support company that operates differently: responsive, transparent, and focused on making the customer’s job easier.

As a former Fire Support Specialist in the U.S. Army, Austin understands the importance of accuracy, coordination, timing, and mission-focused execution. Those principles shape how Bluecrest approaches every requirement, every customer interaction, and every service partner relationship.
